Scrapfall Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Developer: Fernando Tonon (QtMesh)

Scrapfall is a single-player action game available on Android (Google Play), on the web, and on desktop through the QtMesh Games launcher. This policy explains what data the game handles. In short: Scrapfall does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

Data the game stores on your device

  • Game progress – completed missions, best times and scores, unlocked weapons, achievements and settings. Stored only on your device (app storage on Android, browser storage on the web, or the launcher's save folder on desktop).
  • Purchase status (Android) – a local record that the Full Campaign purchase was verified, so the game works offline. It contains no payment details.

You can export, import or reset your progress from the game's Save Data menu, or delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing the site data in your browser.

Data the game does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third-party servers by the game.

In-app purchase (Android)

The optional Full Campaign purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. The game only receives the purchase status from Google Play to unlock content; it never sees your payment information. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Desktop (QtMesh Games / Steam)

When launched from the QtMesh Games launcher, the game reports achievement unlocks and game statistics to the launcher on your own computer, which forwards them to Steam for your Steam account. This is governed by the Valve Privacy Policy.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses when serving the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself sets no cookies.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
